## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data from a telecommunications company to identify churn patterns, key retention drivers, and customer lifetime trends.

The goal is to deliver a **retention analysis dashboard** with data-backed recommendations to help the business reduce customer loss and improve long-term retention

## 📁 Dataset

- **Source:** Telco Customer Churn Dataset — [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn)
- **Size:** 7,043 customers × 21 columns
- **Key columns:** tenure, Contract, MonthlyCharges, TotalCharges, PaymentMethod, Churn (Yes/No)

## 📈 Key Findings

### 🏆 KPIs at a Glance

| Metric                               | Value      |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| Total Customers                      | **7,043**  |
| Churned Customers                    | **1,225**  |
| Churn Rate                           | **17.39%** |
| Average Monthly Revenue per Customer | **$70.50** |

### 📌 Top Insights

1. **1,225 customers** churned during the analysis period, resulting in an overall churn rate of **17.39%**.
2. **Month-to-Month contracts** have the highest churn rate at **37.14%**, making them the most vulnerable customer segment.
3. **Two-Year contracts** have the lowest churn rate at **3.57%**, indicating strong customer loyalty and retention.
4. Customers using **Electronic Check** as their payment method exhibit the highest churn rate at **22.83%**.
5. Customers with longer tenure show significantly lower churn rates, demonstrating that retention improves as customer relationships mature.

---

## 💡 Actionable Recommendations

1. **Promote long-term contracts** — Offer incentives and loyalty discounts to encourage Month-to-Month customers to switch to annual or two-year plans.

2. **Target high-risk customers early** — Implement proactive retention campaigns for customers within their first year of service, when churn risk is highest.

3. **Improve payment experience** — Investigate issues associated with Electronic Check users and encourage migration to more stable payment methods.

4. **Strengthen customer engagement** — Introduce personalized offers, onboarding programs, and regular communication for customers showing signs of disengagement.

5. **Reward loyal customers** — Create exclusive benefits and retention programs for long-tenure customers to further improve lifetime value.
